## Fixes

- Transcripts made with the on-device model no longer scramble the two
  speakers' sentences into each other: speaker turns now stay whole, and
  existing notes are fixed too — just reopen them
- Meetings in languages the live model doesn't support (like Dutch) no
  longer produce gibberish during the call; they're now transcribed right
  after the meeting with the more capable model

## Improvements

- Tidied up the summary-generating placeholder
