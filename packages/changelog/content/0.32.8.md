## New

- The year breakdown in About is now a single chart of notes over time. Hover
  or tab through a year and the count for that year appears above the chart,
  with the totals across the whole span and your busiest year underneath
- Years with no notes are drawn as gaps instead of being skipped, so a quiet
  stretch reads as a quiet stretch rather than two busy years side by side

## Fixes

- Vault activity stays inside the About dialog. The old layout added a row per
  year, so a vault spanning many years pushed the section past the bottom of
  the window. The chart keeps the same height whether you have three years or
  fifty
- The stat tiles now show your tag count, and words transcribed moved up to the
  summary line next to notes and tasks. "In meetings" is now labelled "Meeting
  recordings", which is what the number has always measured
