## Fixes

- Tables now render as real tables in PDF exports. Previously a Markdown table
  came out as raw rows of pipes and dashes. Column alignment and bold header
  cells are kept, and line breaks inside a cell stay on separate lines
- The export dialog now has "Note" ticked by default, so your own writing is
  included alongside the summary unless you turn it off
- The buttons in the app header sit level with the macOS window controls again,
  including when the sidebar is collapsed and in fullscreen, instead of being
  slightly high or low depending on the layout
