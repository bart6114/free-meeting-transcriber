## New

- Images you put in a memo now appear as a row of thumbnails just below the
  title when you're looking at the summary, so a screenshot or a diagram you
  pasted stays in sight without switching back to the memo
- Clicking a thumbnail opens the memo and scrolls to that image
- Images resolve as soon as you paste them, so a freshly pasted screenshot
  shows up in the strip right away
