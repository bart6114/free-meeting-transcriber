## Improvements

- The note timeline in the left sidebar now renders only the rows currently on
  screen instead of every note in your vault. Scrolling, resizing the sidebar,
  and opening the app with thousands of notes are all noticeably lighter
- Everything the timeline does still works the same way: date and tag headers
  stay pinned to the top as you scroll past them, the current-time line stays in
  place, and the "jump to today" and upcoming-meeting chips still take you
  straight there
