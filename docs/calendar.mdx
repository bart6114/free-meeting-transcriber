---
title: "Connect a calendar"
description: "Bring upcoming meetings into Free Meeting Transcriber and start them from one place."
---

Open **Calendar** in the sidebar, add an account, and enable the calendars you want Free Meeting Transcriber to show. Connected events appear in the calendar and timeline.

Free Meeting Transcriber uses calendar events for titles, times, participants, reminders, and meeting links. It does not add a bot to your calls.

Google and Outlook connections are read-only. Free Meeting Transcriber reads calendar and event details to show upcoming events and associate them with your notes. It does not create, edit, or delete events in Google Calendar or Outlook.

## Apple Calendar

Apple Calendar is available on macOS.

1. Choose **Apple Calendar**.
2. Allow Calendar access when macOS asks.
3. Enable the calendars you want to see.

If access was denied, open **Settings → Permissions** in Free Meeting Transcriber. Use **Open** beside Calendar to change the macOS permission.

## Google Calendar

1. Choose **Google Calendar**, then finish authorization in your browser.
2. Return to Free Meeting Transcriber and enable the calendars you want to sync.

## Outlook Calendar

1. Choose **Outlook Calendar**, then finish authorization in your browser.
2. Return to Free Meeting Transcriber and enable the calendars you want to sync.

## Manage or delete connected calendar data

To control which calendars appear in Free Meeting Transcriber, open **Calendar**, expand the provider, and turn individual calendars on or off.

To disconnect Google Calendar or Outlook Calendar:

1. Open **Calendar** and expand **Google** or **Outlook**.
2. Hover over or right-click the connected account heading.
3. Open **•••** and choose **Disconnect**.

Disconnecting stops future access and syncs. After Free Meeting Transcriber refreshes, the account's cached calendars and events disappear from active calendar views. Your notes and memos remain, including event context already associated with a note.

You can also revoke Free Meeting Transcriber's access from your [Google Account connections](https://myaccount.google.com/connections), the [Microsoft My Apps portal](https://myapplications.microsoft.com) for a work or school account, or [Microsoft account app access](https://account.live.com/consent/Manage) for a personal account.

To permanently remove calendar-derived data retained on a Mac, you currently need to remove all local Free Meeting Transcriber app data from that Mac:

1. Export anything you want to keep, then quit Free Meeting Transcriber.
2. In Finder, choose **Go → Go to Folder**.
3. Move this folder to Trash if it exists:
   - `~/Library/Application Support/free-meeting-transcriber`
4. Empty Trash before reopening Free Meeting Transcriber.

This removes Free Meeting Transcriber's local database and settings on that Mac, including cached calendar data and event context stored with local records. It does not delete files you exported or copies you chose to sync or share.

<Note>
  Apple Calendar uses the calendar accounts already connected to your Mac.
</Note>

If events are missing, refresh the calendar list, confirm the calendar is enabled, and reconnect any account marked **Reconnect required**.
