---
title: "Data, privacy, and retention"
description: "Understand what stays on your computer, what can leave it, and how long audio is kept."
---

Free Meeting Transcriber stores its app data on your computer and captures meetings without adding a bot to the call. Data leaves your computer only when you use a connected feature that needs it.

## What stays on your computer

Your notes, local transcripts, settings, recordings you choose to keep, and downloaded local models are stored locally. You can write notes, record audio, and use configured local models without sending meeting content anywhere.

Your data lives in plain files in the vault folder you chose during setup. You can edit those files with other tools; Free Meeting Transcriber picks up external changes and moves the previous version to the vault's `.trash/` folder before overwriting anything. Use the export tools when you need a portable copy.

## When content is sent elsewhere

Transcription always runs on your computer. A remote **Intelligence** provider receives the text and instructions needed to create a summary.

Your own API-key providers and local providers are separate choices, and API keys are stored in the macOS keychain, not in the vault. Review the provider's own retention and training policies before sending sensitive content.

## Keep a meeting fully local

1. Select an on-device model under **Settings → Transcription**.
2. Select LM Studio or Ollama under **Settings → Intelligence**.
3. Avoid remote API-key providers for that content.

See [Use Free Meeting Transcriber offline](/offline) for the full setup.

## Choose how long audio is kept

Open **Settings → App → Meetings → Audio file retention**. Choose:

- **Don't save**
- **1 day**
- **3 days**
- **1 week**
- **1 month**
- **Forever**

Deleting retained audio does not remove the meeting note or transcript. If you need the recording later, open the meeting's **•••** menu, choose **Show in Finder**, and copy the audio before its retention period ends.

## Usage analytics

This build of Free Meeting Transcriber never sends usage analytics or telemetry, regardless of the **Share usage data** setting under **Settings → App**.

<Warning>
  Recording-consent rules depend on where you and the other participants are located. Make sure you have permission before recording.
</Warning>
