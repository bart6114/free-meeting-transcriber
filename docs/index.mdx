I wanted a meeting notetaker that would transcribe locally, keep my notes in ordinary files, and stay out of the way. Free Meeting Transcriber is the result.

It records your microphone and system audio without sending a bot into the call. There is no account, subscription, cloud backend, or telemetry. Your vault stays on your Mac and remains useful outside the app. If you want generated summaries, connect your own hosted provider or a local model.

## The short version

1. Create a note.
2. Click **Record**.
3. Write anything you want to remember in **Memos**.
4. Click **Stop listening** and review the transcript.
5. If you configured Intelligence, review the generated summary.
