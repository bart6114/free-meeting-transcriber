## The short version

1. Create a note.
2. Click **Record**.
3. Take rough notes in **Memos** while Free Meeting Transcriber listens.
4. Stop listening, then review **Summary** and **Transcript**.

Your app data is stored locally. If you choose a remote language-model provider, the content needed for that feature is sent to that provider.

<Note>
  New to Free Meeting Transcriber? Open the **Welcome to Free Meeting Transcriber** note, click **Record**, and say a few sentences out loud to see transcription in action.
</Note>
