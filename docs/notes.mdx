Every meeting note has three kinds of views:

- **Memos** are the notes you write yourself.
- **Summary** is the structured note generated by your Intelligence model.
- **Transcript** is the captured conversation.

You can edit memos and summaries like regular notes. In a transcript, click a speaker label to rename it.

## Shape the summary

Use the template picker beside **Summary** to change its structure. You can also customize the **Auto** summary prompt and add names and specialized terms to your dictionary.

See [Customize meeting summaries](/customize-summaries) for the complete setup.

## Export a meeting

1. Open the **•••** menu in the meeting header.
2. Choose **Export**.
3. Select PDF, text, Markdown, or Org format.
4. Include any combination of the memo, summary, and transcript.

Free Meeting Transcriber writes the export to your Downloads folder and reveals the file when it is ready.
