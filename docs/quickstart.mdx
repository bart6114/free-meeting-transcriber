---
title: "Your first meeting"
description: "Set up Free Meeting Transcriber and test it before your next call."
---

<Steps>
  <Step title="Install Free Meeting Transcriber">
    Build the app from the [GitHub repository](https://github.com/bart6114/free-meeting-transcriber), open it, and follow the setup screen.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Allow audio access">
    Allow **Microphone** so Free Meeting Transcriber can hear you. Allow **System Audio** so it can hear everyone else on the call. **Accessibility** is optional and helps detect meeting apps.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose where your data lives">
    Pick the folder where Free Meeting Transcriber stores its local data. Everything stays in plain files on your computer.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set up your models">
    Download the on-device transcription model, then choose an Intelligence provider — a local server like LM Studio or Ollama, or your own API key — for summaries.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Try it out">
    Open the **Welcome to Free Meeting Transcriber** note, click **Record** in the top-right corner, and say a few sentences out loud. Stop listening, then open **Memos**, **Summary**, and **Transcript** to see what was captured.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Record a real meeting

Create a note, then click **Record** in the top-right corner. See [Record meetings](/meetings) for the full flow.

If Free Meeting Transcriber cannot hear the call, start with [audio troubleshooting](/troubleshooting#audio-is-missing).
