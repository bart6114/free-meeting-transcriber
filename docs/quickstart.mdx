---
title: "Your first meeting"
description: "Set up Free Meeting Transcriber and try a private demo before your next call."
---

<Steps>
  <Step title="Install Free Meeting Transcriber">
    Build the app from the [GitHub repository](https://github.com/bart6114/free-meeting-transcriber), open it, and follow the setup screen.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Allow audio access">
    Allow **Microphone** so Free Meeting Transcriber can hear you. Allow **System Audio** so it can hear everyone else on the call.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Finish the optional setup">
    Connect your calendars if you want meeting-aware notes. Choose where Free Meeting Transcriber stores its local data.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Run the private demo">
    Open **Welcome to Free Meeting Transcriber** and click **Join & record**. The app opens a prerecorded meeting and treats it like a real call.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the result">
    When the video ends, return to the app and stop listening. Open **Memos**, **Summary**, and **Transcript** to see what was captured.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Record a real meeting

Open a calendar event or create a note, then click **Join & record** or **Record** in the top-right corner. See [Record meetings](/meetings) for the full flow.

If Free Meeting Transcriber cannot hear the call, start with [audio troubleshooting](/troubleshooting#audio-is-missing).
